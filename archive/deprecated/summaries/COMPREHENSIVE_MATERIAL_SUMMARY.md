# Comprehensive Material Summary: ResearchProcess-GPS

## Overview

After reading all documentation in the ResearchProcess-GPS directory, this represents a revolutionary genealogical research platform that fundamentally reimagines how genealogical research is conducted, documented, and shared. The project has evolved far beyond traditional genealogy software to become a comprehensive research methodology platform.

## Core Revolutionary Concepts

### 1. Paradigm Shift: Process Over Conclusions
**Traditional Software**: Person → Family → Events → Sources  
**ResearchProcess-GPS**: Question → Hypothesis → Evidence → Analysis → Conclusion → Person (maybe)

The system prioritizes documenting the research journey, not just the endpoints, enabling:
- GPS (Genealogical Proof Standard) compliance by design
- Hypothesis testing and theory versioning
- Negative research documentation as first-class citizens
- Complete preservation of reasoning chains

### 2. Identity vs. Person Revolution
**Identity System**: Manages "mystery people" with fragmentary evidence
- Multiple identities that may represent the same person
- Progressive identity resolution with confidence scoring
- Persona extraction from individual sources
- Smart clustering and correlation engines
- Support for conflicting evidence and uncertain relationships

### 3. Flexible Link System Beyond GEDCOM
Goes far beyond traditional family relationships to support:
- Uncertain connections ("possibly_same_person", "probably_not_same")
- Complex social networks ("business_partner", "witnessed_document_for")
- DNA-specific relationships with triangulation support
- Geographic and migration connections
- Theoretical and hypothesis-based relationships
