## Professional Standards Organizations: Current Challenges and Evolution

### Board for Certification of Genealogists (BCG)

#### Recent Major Changes (2024-2025)
- **New Two-Part Certification Process**: Effective July 1, 2025, BCG implemented a new application process to address issues with the previous system where applicants had to invest significant time in all six parts before knowing if their portfolio was "premature"
- **Updated Ethics Code**: March 11, 2025 - Updated to address DNA evidence protection and new terminology related to genetic genealogy
- **Processing Time Challenges**: Despite process improvements, evaluation times remain lengthy due to high submission volumes

#### Ongoing Professional Requirements
- **Recertification Every 5 Years**: Associates must submit projects demonstrating continued skill development and improvement on previous shortfalls
- **GPS Compliance Mandatory**: All work must meet the Genealogical Proof Standard's five elements
- **Documentation Standards**: Complete source citation for every fact not common knowledge

### Association of Professional Genealogists (APG)

#### Current Focus Areas (2024)
- **Workflow Optimization**: September 2024 APG Quarterly discussed changing workflows to increase productivity
- **Technology Integration**: Heavy emphasis on incorporating DNA into professional workflows for historical research and lineage society work
- **Business Management**: Ongoing challenges with "communicating with clients, writing reports, setting fees, using DNA in business, maintaining business records, developing marketing strategies"

#### Professional Development Priorities
- **Virtual Professional Management Conference (PMC)**: Focus on career navigation and professional skill development
- **Collaborative Education**: Study groups and special interest groups for both aspiring and established professionals
- **Technology Tools**: Text expanders, business software evaluation, and productivity tools for genealogy businesses

### National Genealogical Society (NGS)

#### 2025 Conference Focus
- **"Tales and Trails" Theme**: Emphasis on storytelling and migration patterns, particularly American migration history
- **Diversity Initiatives**: Partnership with AAHGS for African American family history research and lecturers of color
- **Post-Pandemic Adaptation**: Moving to create "new event experience as community" due to industry-wide challenges

#### Professional Education
- **Advanced Skills Certificate Course**: "Today's complex genealogical challenges demand mastery of both digital and traditional research environments"
- **NGSQ Scholarly Standards**: Emphasis on compiled genealogies, case studies, methodology essays, and previously unpublished source materials
- **Guidelines for Standards**: Recommended practices for improving skills and performance in genealogical pursuits

## Current Software and Technology Limitations

### Critical Data Integrity Issues

#### Major Data Loss Incidents (2024-2025)
- **RootsMagic 10 (December 2024)**: Data loss bug where information entered after generating Relationship Charts was lost upon program closure
- **Software MacKiev Breach**: 25GB of data from 60,000 Family Tree Maker users exposed through misconfigured Elasticsearch server
- **Performance Issues**: Heredis "painfully slow on Windows," GRAMPS "crashes at almost every session"

#### Ongoing GEDCOM Problems
- **Unicode Support**: Many suppliers still haven't fully supported multilingual Unicode text from GEDCOM 5.5 (1996), limiting international character sets
- **GEDCOM 7.0 Adoption**: Despite 2021 release, GEDCOM 5.5.1 remains industry standard with slow adoption
- **Data Loss During Transfer**: "Most commonly found fault leading to data loss was the failure to read the NOTE tag at all possible levels"

### Professional Workflow Gaps

#### Standards Compliance Issues
- **GPS Documentation**: No software adequately supports documenting the complete research process required by GPS
- **Research Log Integration**: Professional research logs remain separate from family tree data in all major platforms
- **Evidence-Conclusion Separation**: Current software fails to properly distinguish between evidence and conclusions
- **Peer Review Workflows**: No collaborative review processes for professional genealogy work

#### Business Management Limitations
- **Client Project Management**: No integrated tools for managing genealogy client projects with time tracking and billing
- **Professional Report Generation**: Limited ability to generate reports meeting BCG/NGS professional standards
- **Attribution and Version Control**: Inadequate tracking of research contributions and decision-making processes
- **Quality Assurance**: No systematic approaches to verify research thoroughness and accuracy
