# Genealogical Work Products Analysis

## Overview
Professional genealogists create specific work products that document their research process and conclusions. These products serve both as personal research tools and as deliverables to clients or for publication.

## Primary Work Products

### 1. Research Log
**Purpose**: Document all searches conducted, including negative results
**Components**:
- Date of search
- Repository/database searched  
- Search parameters/terms used
- Time period covered
- Results summary (found/not found)
- Next steps identified
- Document/film numbers accessed

**Professional Standards**: 
- Board for Certification of Genealogists (BCG) requires research logs
- Format varies but content requirements are standard

### 2. Research Plan/Strategy
**Purpose**: Outline future research objectives and methodology
**Components**:
- Research question/objective
- Known facts summary
- Hypotheses to test
- Sources to consult
- Priority order
- Time/cost estimates

### 3. Proof Argument/Statement
**Purpose**: Document reasoning that leads from evidence to conclusion
**Types**:
- **Proof Summary**: Brief statement when evidence is direct and uncontested
- **Proof Argument**: Detailed narrative for complex or conflicting evidence

**Components**:
- Research question addressed
- Evidence presented (with citations)
- Analysis of each piece of evidence
- Resolution of conflicts
- Conclusion with reasoning

### 4. Evidence Analysis Worksheet
**Purpose**: Systematic evaluation of sources and information
**Components**:
- Source citation
- Information extracted
- Evidence type (direct/indirect/negative)
- Source evaluation (original/derivative)
- Information evaluation (primary/secondary)
- Evidence quality assessment
- Relevance to research question

### 5. Research Report
**Purpose**: Formal presentation of research findings
**Components**:
- Executive summary
- Research objectives
- Methodology
- Findings (organized by family/individual)
- Evidence summaries
- Conclusions
- Recommendations for further research
- Full source citations
- Document copies (appendices)

### 6. Correlation/Timeline Analysis
**Purpose**: Organize evidence chronologically to identify patterns
**Components**:
- Date/time period
- Event description
- Person(s) involved
- Source citation
- Location
- Analysis notes

### 7. Correspondence Log
**Purpose**: Track research correspondence and responses
**Components**:
- Date sent/received
- Recipient/sender
- Subject/purpose
- Response status
- Follow-up needed
- Results obtained

### 8. Source Abstract/Extract
**Purpose**: Capture source information in condensed form
**Types**:
- **Abstract**: Summary of relevant information
- **Extract**: Exact copy of relevant portions
- **Transcription**: Complete exact copy

**Components**:
- Full source citation
- Abstract/extract/transcription content
- Researcher notes/analysis
- Date created
- Relevance markers

### 9. Family Group Analysis
**Purpose**: Compile all evidence about a family unit
**Components**:
- Parents' information with evidence
- Children's information with evidence  
- Chronological event list
- Evidence conflicts noted
- Analysis and conclusions
- Further research needed

### 10. Pedigree Analysis
**Purpose**: Evaluate ancestral lines for completeness and accuracy
**Components**:
- Generation-by-generation analysis
- Evidence quality assessment per person
- Research status indicators
- Problem areas identified
- Research recommendations

## Work Product Relationships

### Evidence Chain
```
Source → Extract/Abstract → Evidence Analysis → Proof Argument → Conclusion
                ↓                    ↓
          Research Log         Hypothesis Testing
```

### Research Workflow
```
Research Question → Research Plan → Research Log → Evidence Collection
         ↓                                              ↓
    Hypothesis                                   Evidence Analysis
         ↓                                              ↓
    Testing/Revision ←────────────────────────── Proof Development
         ↓
    Conclusion → Research Report
```

## Data Requirements

### Essential Attributes
1. **Versioning**: Work products evolve over time
2. **Dating**: Creation and revision dates
3. **Attribution**: Who created/modified
4. **Linking**: Connect to people, sources, other work products
5. **Status**: Draft, reviewed, final, superseded
6. **Access Control**: Private, shared, public

### Storage Considerations
1. **Text Format**: Most are narrative/structured text
2. **Templates**: Standard formats for consistency
3. **Attachments**: Supporting documents, images
4. **Cross-References**: Links between related items
5. **Export Formats**: PDF, Word, HTML for sharing

## Integration with Genealogy Software

### Current State
- Most software focuses on conclusion data
- Limited research process documentation
- No standard exchange format for work products
- Research logs often kept separately

### Desired Capabilities
1. Create/edit work products within software
2. Link work products to relevant data
3. Template-based creation
4. Export for professional presentation
5. Import/export between systems
6. Collaborative editing features

## Professional Standards Compliance

### BCG Standards Elements
- Research planning documentation
- Thorough search documentation  
- Source citation completeness
- Evidence analysis documentation
- Proof argument requirements

### Legal/Ethical Considerations
- Client confidentiality markers
- Copyright notation for sources
- Privacy flags for living persons
- Audit trail for changes
