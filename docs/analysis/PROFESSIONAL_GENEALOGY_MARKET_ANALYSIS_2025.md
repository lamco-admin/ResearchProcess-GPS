## Professional Standards Evolution

- **Certified Genetic Genealogist (CGG) Credential:** First new credential in decades
- **Two-Part Certification Process:** Streamlined application starting July 2025
- **DNA Privacy Standards:** Updated genealogist code for DNA sample protection

**Impact Assessment:** BCG demonstrates highest adaptation to market changes with formal recognition of genetic genealogy as distinct specialization.
